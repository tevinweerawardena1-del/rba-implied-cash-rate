How it's calculated

1. Futures give monthly averages

Each ASX 30 Day Interbank Cash Rate Futures contract settles on the average interbank overnight cash rate over its calendar month, so 100 minus the price is the market's expected average rate for that month.

2. Convert to cash rate target terms

The overnight rate trades a few basis points away from the RBA's target. Today's gap (–) is subtracted from every contract.

3. Back out the rate after each meeting

A new target applies from the day after the decision. If the month after the meeting has no meeting of its own, its contract gives the post-meeting rate directly. Otherwise, with D days in the month and d₁ days before the change:

r_after = (monthly average × D − r_before × d₁) ÷ (D − d₁)

Each meeting's result becomes the next meeting's r_before. The futures run about 17 months out, so the path stops there.
